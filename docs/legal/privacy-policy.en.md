# Els App Privacy Policy

**Version 3.0**
**Applies to:** Els App (iOS, Android and web at elsapp.eccchurch.global), the ECC Portal, CKids, monitor screens and the eccchurch.global website
**Previous:** Version 2.0 — 23 August 2026

---

## Summary

- We collect the information we need to serve you: identity and contact details, family, service/event attendance, homecell and in-app activity.
- Because Els App is a church app, your information may reveal your **religious beliefs**. We treat it as information requiring special protection and only process it with your consent.
- We **do not sell** your data and **do not use it for advertising**.
- You can **view, correct, download and request deletion** of your data, and control who can see it.
- Children's data is managed only by parents/guardians.
- Contact our Data Protection Officer at **privacy@eccchurch.global**.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
| Type | Examples | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Identity & contact | Full name, mobile number (WhatsApp), email, gender, date of birth, address, church branch | Name, mobile number and branch are required; others are optional |
| Photos | Profile photo, family photos | Optional |
| Family | Spouse, children, parents and basic details of family members you add | Optional |
| Ministry & community | Homecell, group and ministry team membership, serving schedules | Depends on participation |
| Prayer requests | Content of your prayer request (can be sent anonymously) | Optional |
| Personal notes | Bible 365 reflection notes, Bible bookmarks | Optional, visible only to you |
| Event payments | Transfer receipts you upload | Paid events only |
| Other people's data | Names and mobile numbers of **guests** you invite or record (e.g. homecell guests) | Optional — see Section 8 |

### 2.2 Information recorded when you use our services
- **Attendance & reservations:** service reservations, check-ins (swipe, QR scan or by an usher), event and homecell attendance, CKids pickup codes.
- **Account & security:** time and method of login (WhatsApp OTP or email link), login sessions, admin activity logs (audit log).
- **Anonymous analytics:** screens opened, time on screen, app response times, device type (iOS/Android/web) and app version. This uses a **random per-device code** and is **not linked** to your name, number or account. You can turn it off in **Settings → Privacy**.
- **Error reports:** technical information when the app has a problem (version, device, error message) so we can fix it.

### 2.3 Device permissions (only when you use the feature)
| Permission | Used for | Stored? |
|---|---|---|
| Location (while using the app) | Confirming you are at the venue for self check-in; finding the nearest homecell | **No** — only used to calculate distance at that moment |
| Camera | Scanning QR codes | No |
| Photo library | Choosing photos to upload; saving images you share | Only photos you upload |
| Calendar | Adding events to your calendar (the app **never reads** your calendar) | No |
| Notifications | Bible 365 reminders on your device | No |

### 2.4 What we do not collect
- Your device contacts.
- Credit/debit card numbers (payments are handled by banks/payment providers).
- Health information as a category. However, prayer requests you write may contain personal information — please share only what you are comfortable with.
- **Biometric data:** face login has been **discontinued**. Face data previously stored by that feature has been permanently deleted. Face ID/fingerprint on your device is processed by the operating system and never sent to us.

## 3. Information requiring special protection

We apply stricter handling to:
- **Religious beliefs** — your church membership and participation. In Malaysia and Australia this is *sensitive information*.
- **Children's data** — see Section 7.
- **Financial data** — event payment receipts.
- **Prayer request content** — may contain personal information.

This information is processed only with your **explicit consent**, is visible only to authorised people, and is not shared beyond ministry purposes.

## 5. Legal basis

- **Consent** — when you create an account, add family/children's data or use optional features. We ask for explicit consent for religious/sensitive information.
- **Providing the service (contract)** — to deliver features you request (e.g. reservations, check-in).
- **Legitimate interests** — church administration, system security and anonymous analytics, balanced against your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — where required by law.

You may **withdraw consent** at any time (see Section 10). Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, but some features may no longer be available.

---

## 6. Who can see your information

- **You** — all of your information.
- **Other members** — only what you allow in **Settings → Privacy** (e.g. family only, homecell, or no one).
- **Homecell leaders / area PICs / ministry staff** — what they need for their role (e.g. homecell attendance, CKids attendance).
- **Church admins & full-timers** — according to their role permissions (*Role Access*); every access and change is logged.
- **Monitor screens & reports** — show **aggregate numbers only**, without names or numbers.

---

## 7. Children

- Children **under 13** cannot create accounts. Their data is added and managed only by a **parent/guardian** through the Family menu.
- Users aged **13 to under 18** may use their own account only **with parent/guardian consent**, ideally linked to a family account.
- CKids data (name, date of birth, attendance, points, pickup code) is visible only to parents/guardians and authorised CKids staff.
- Parents/guardians can view, correct and request deletion of their child's data at any time.
- You can report misuse or inappropriate content involving children to **support@eccchurch.global**.

---

## 8. Guests and other people's information

When you register **guests** (e.g. inviting a friend to a service/event or recording homecell guests), please make sure they **know about and agree to** their name and mobile number being given to the church. Guest data is used for attendance records and pastoral follow-up, and guests may ask for deletion at privacy@eccchurch.global.

**Guest-mode** users (without an account) who ask to join a homecell provide their name, mobile number and optionally gender/age/message. This is shared only with the leader of that homecell.

## 10. Your rights

| Right | How |
|---|---|
| **Be informed** about processing | This policy, or ask privacy@eccchurch.global |
| **Access & correct** your data | Profile / Edit Profile / Family in the app |
| **Receive a copy (portability)** | Settings → Privacy → **Download my data** (JSON file), or email privacy@eccchurch.global |
| **Delete your data / account** | Settings → **Delete Account** (account deactivated), or request **permanent deletion** at privacy@eccchurch.global |
| **Withdraw consent** | Settings → Privacy (e.g. analytics), or email us |
| **Control who sees your data** | Settings → **Privacy** |
| **Stop non-essential WhatsApp/email** (reminders, greetings, announcements) | Settings → Privacy → **WhatsApp messages**, or email privacy@eccchurch.global; security messages (OTP) are still sent |
| **Object or complain** | See Section 14 |

**Response times:** we respond **within 3×24 hours** for users in Indonesia as required by the PDP Law, within **21 days** for access requests in Malaysia, and within **30 days** in Australia. We may need to verify your identity first.

## 12. Security

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Password-free login via WhatsApp OTP or email link; short-lived sessions with secure token refresh.
- Role-based access (*Role Access*) — people only see what their role requires.
- Activity logs for every admin change.
- Login rate limiting and system monitoring.
- Credential vault contents and regular backups are stored **encrypted (AES-256)** with restricted access.

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect your account is being misused, contact support@eccchurch.global immediately.

---

## 13. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will:
- notify **you and the Indonesian authority within 3×24 hours** of becoming aware of it;
- notify the **Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours** where required, and affected users;
- notify the **OAIC (Australia)** and affected individuals **as soon as practicable** for eligible data breaches;
- explain what data was affected, what we have done, and steps you can take.
